The Menu

Ancient craft  ·  Present pleasure

Philosophy

Every dish is a conversation between the ancient medina and the present moment.

Our menu is shaped by the season and the market — sourced from small producers across Morocco and the spice traders of Marrakech Medina. It changes. It breathes. Return often.

A Moroccan Ending

Every meal ends
with tea

The pouring of mint tea is not a formality at Dar Zafran. It is the moment when dinner becomes conversation — when the table belongs to you alone. Gunpowder green tea, fresh spearmint, and a pour from high enough to raise a foam. Some rituals are not improvable.
