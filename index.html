Our Story

Born from
the medina's
ancient kitchens

Dar Zafran was built around a single conviction: that the recipes of the Moroccan kitchen deserve the same care and attention as any great culinary tradition.

A restored riad in the heart of Marrakech's Medina. The architecture carries its own history. The recipes are living. The presentation has been refined for the contemporary table.
